# Sketchfab history models

48 models for history topics (the owner's choices of 21 Nov 2026, from a sheet of 61 shortlisted by S4): museum scans under **CC0** (The Hunt Museum, LWL-Archaeologie, the Cleveland and Minneapolis museums, Musée Saint-Raymond, the National Museum of Antiquities in Leiden, the Virtual Museums of Małopolska and others) and modellers' reconstructions under **CC BY 4.0**, each as its Sketchfab page said when the owner's Chrome downloaded it on 21 November 2026. Three more the owner chose were left out because they don't survive reduction to their section's budget (the Ballyscullion cauldron, LWL's oil lamp and a Viking longhouse with 84 thatch pictures), and four more were left out on the owner's choice of no swords or shields (three swords and the Yetholm shield).

**These are prepared copies, not the downloads.** The 55 downloads came to 661 MB, so each file here is its download with its glTF `extras` stripped and reduced to its section's budget by storystage2's own `reduceModel` (props 8,000 triangles, set 20,000, pictures at most 1,024 px, as WebP), which the build would do anyway. The table gives each download's SHA-256 and the prepared file's. The downloads are kept outside every repository (`Dropbox/6 - IT/dev/downloads/history-sketchfab`, named by Sketchfab id).

The build credits each "<title> by <maker> (CC0), via Sketchfab", or "<title> by <maker>, CC BY 4.0, via Sketchfab, changed for StoryStage" (storystage2's `docs/legal/licence-audit.md`).
