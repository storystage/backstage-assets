# Solar System Scope originals

The pictures `sources/solar-system.json` makes the Sun, the Moon and the planets from (storystage2's `tools/backstage-sources/src/globe.ts`; the owner's choice of 21 Nov 2026): Solar System Scope's 2K textures, from [solarsystemscope.com/textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), whose page says they are "Distributed under Attribution 4.0 International license" and may be used, adapted and shared for any purpose, even commercially. Each model is credited to Solar System Scope under **CC BY 4.0** and says it was changed for StoryStage (storystage2's `docs/legal/licence-audit.md`).

Fetched on 21 November 2026 from `https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/<file>` (the site allows robots), byte for byte, and kept here so the build fetches exactly what was reviewed, pinned to the commit that added them. Venus is its cloud tops (`venus_atmosphere`), as it looks; Saturn has its ring as a second picture with transparency.
